Our Journey

From a Small Office to the City's Most Trusted Agency

Gundu Real Estate is built on trust, transparency, and a commitment to helping people make confident property decisions. Serving the communities of Gundu, Bhaktapur, Kathmandu, and Lalitpur, we help people explore opportunities in land, houses, and real estate.

Trusted by 5,000+ people, our focus is on building lasting relationships, understanding each client's needs, and providing reliable support throughout their property journey. Whether you are looking to buy land, sell a house, or invest in real estate, Gundu Real Estate is here to help you take the next step with confidence.
